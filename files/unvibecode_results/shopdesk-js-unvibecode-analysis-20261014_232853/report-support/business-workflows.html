Workflow 1

order creation and fulfillment

What this workflow does

authenticated customer submits /api/orders to create an order, reserve inventory, charge payment, and dispatch shipping.

Purpose

purchase a product with selected payment and shipping method

What it produces

order

Who relies on it

customer, inventory subsystem, payment processor, shipping subsystem

Why this workflow matters

  • creates a paid order record
  • reduces stock when enabled
  • captures payment authorization/charge outcome
  • produces a shipping tracking identifier or failure response

Workflow 3

payment reconciliation and event fan-out

What this workflow does

payment gateway webhook verifies signature, updates paid order state, writes a ledger credit, and emits downstream events.

Purpose

confirm a gateway payment result and record its accounting impact

What it produces

payment webhook event / ledger entry

Who relies on it

order record, ledger, notification listener

Why this workflow matters

  • confirms payment-linked order status
  • creates accounting credit row
  • fans out follow-on order.payed event to listeners
